Christopher Rapalo is a filmmaker and photographer based out of Miami, Florida. Rapalo began his professional work with Third Dream Media, a digital agency founded by him in 2014, he later co produced events in Wynwood, FL during key events like Art Basel and moderated panels alongside Dell and Intel. Rapalo's debut documentary 'Retro Couture,' (2016) explores the world of fashion in Miami as told through five local pioneers in the industry inspired by vintage pieces. The documentary takes a look at Miami's history as a clothing manufacturer, and touches on it's future as the next fashion capital of the world.
