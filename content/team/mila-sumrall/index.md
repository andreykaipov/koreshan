Mila Sumrall is an artist from West Palm Beach, Florida. She attended college at Florida State University, where she graduated magna cum laude with her BA in Theatre on both the performance and production design tracks. ​

She has a diverse skillset, which includes acting, writing, directing, lighting design and SFX makeup. ​

When she is not working on a production, she loves learning about natural sciences like mycology and entomology!
