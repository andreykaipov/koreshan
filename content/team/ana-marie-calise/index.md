Ana Marie Calise is a Portuguese- American actor and director. She graduated from NYU Tisch with a BFA in Drama and recently graduated from FAU with her MFA in performance. She is currently pursuing her PhD in Theatre and Nonprofit Management. Ana spent numerous years working as an actor, director, and writer in NYC and currently resides full time South Florida . Recent acting credits include Mrs. Phagan in Parade (Slowburn), Mags in Painting Churches (Pigs Do Fly) Rachel/Amanda in A Shonda (The Foundry) and May in Fool For Love (Think Now Theatre Company). Recent directing credits include: Lend Me a Soprano (Lynn) Firebringer (FAU) Theatre Lab's New Play Festival (FAU), La Cage Aux Folles (Stadium Theatre), and Thanksgiving Eve (Miami One Acts).
