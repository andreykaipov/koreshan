Sage Kirk, originally from upstate New York, is a dedicated SFX Makeup Artist always seeking to go above and beyond. She graduated from Cosmix, one in four accredited schools for special effects makeup in the United States. From beauty and high fashion to creature creation and fantasy, her goal is to bring your vision to life. Pushing past the limits with avant-garde expressions and out of the box interpretations, Sage takes a creative approach at any given opportunity.
