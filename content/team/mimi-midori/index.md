**Mimi Midori** is a visual director with over seven years of experience creating music videos, commercials, films, and more. She has experience working successfully with both businesses and other creatives. Her winning strategy is to form a close bond with her clients, helping them execute their vision and tell their stories. 

Mimi was born and raised in Miami, FL, where she studied Film, Television & Digital Production at Miami Dade College. There she gained hands-on experience behind the camera and broadened her expertise as an effective Director. 

Mimi fell in love with storytelling from a young age, using her writing to express her artistic, imaginative, and creative nature. When she was introduced to a video camera, not only was she able to write the stories playing in her head, she was now able to show them too.
