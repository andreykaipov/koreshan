What must a sister do to bring forth the reincarnation of her BrotherMaster?

Emma, played by the marvelous [@actressmarcelaherrera](https://www.instagram.com/actressmarcelaherrera/) , is the stoic, practical leader of her community following her brother, Cyrus Teed’s death. She must manage to maintain her faith while the Koreshans await the prophesied future.
