Since releasing the teaser for “Last Days of Koreshan,
” I want to introduce myself a little bit! Hi, I
am L.Fried, the writer/director/producer of the short film that is inspired by a true Florida Story.
This is my second timing directing a narrative film, my first being *I Know How You Like Your
Coffee*. I have produced/directed many non-fiction pieces, as well as a couple of music videos
and experimental shorts (“Granny’s Living Room” and “Warmth”). Other roles I tend to take on in
production are within the art department (production design and set dressing) or post-production (editing, marketing, etc).
*Last Days* is my first foray into creating a film based on a historical moment and it has been
such an incredibly fun journey. Taking on a project at this scale was also something I thought
was completely out of my wheelhouse, but it’s been such a rewarding and challenging process
every step of the way. I really cannot describe the high that followed the production days, and as
I fine tune the edit—I am really excited to share a Work-in-Progress screening this fall.
What began as a visual of a dead cult leader surrounded by his followers days after he passed
away, gave way to curiosity whethe[\#r](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/r/) there were disillusions, doubts, or those who remained
unwaveringly faithful. Last Days of Koreshan is meant to poke and prod at belief systems that
leave no room for doubt or questioning. To fit the motif, the screening will give you an
opportunity to voice your thoughts and feedback on the unfinished film, and feature a discussion
between myself and Lyn Millner, the author of *The Allure of Immortality: An American Cult, a
Florida Swamp, and a Renegade Prophet*, THE historical book on Cyrus Teed and the
Koreshan Unity. Her book truly provided a backbone to my research on the Florida Cult. I hope
to see you all at the WIP Screening September 18 at the Sample McDougald House (where
principle filming took place)!
